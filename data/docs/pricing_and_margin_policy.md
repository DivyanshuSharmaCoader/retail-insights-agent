# Kestrel Retail Pricing and Margin Policy

*Kestrel Retail is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and exists to demonstrate retrieval.*

## Target gross margin by category

Gross margin is (net revenue - cost of goods) / net revenue. Category teams manage prices so that the monthly gross margin stays inside these bands:

| Category | Target gross margin |
|---|---|
| Dairy | 18% - 22% |
| Snacks | 35% - 40% |
| Beverages | 38% - 42% |
| Personal Care | 42% - 48% |
| Household | 33% - 37% |
| Fresh Produce | 25% - 30% |

A category that falls below its band for two months in a row needs a margin recovery plan.

## Price changes

- Permanent price changes of up to 10% can be approved by the category buyer.
- Changes above 10% need approval from the Category Head.
- Prices are changed only on Mondays, so that shelf labels and the app update together.

## Markdowns for near-expiry stock

To reduce waste, near-expiry chilled and fresh products are marked down automatically:

- **25% off** when 3 days of shelf life remain.
- **50% off** on the last day of shelf life.
- Products past their expiry date are removed and recorded as shrinkage.

Markdowns are separate from promotions and do not count towards a promotion's uplift.

## Competitor price checks

Price checks against the two main local competitors are carried out every two weeks for the top 200 SKUs. Kestrel aims to be within 3% of the lowest competitor price on these "key value items".
