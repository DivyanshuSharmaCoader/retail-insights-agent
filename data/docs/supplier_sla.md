# Kestrel Retail Supplier Service Level Agreement (summary)

*Kestrel Retail is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and exists to demonstrate retrieval.*

## Scope

This summary applies to every supplier that delivers to Kestrel distribution centres or directly to stores. The full contract with each supplier takes precedence where it differs.

## Service levels

| Measure | Target | How it is measured |
|---|---|---|
| Fill rate | at least **95%** | units delivered / units ordered, per month |
| On time in full (OTIF) | at least **92%** | deliveries that arrive in the agreed slot with the full quantity |
| Lead time, chilled (Dairy) | 24 hours | order to delivery |
| Lead time, Fresh Produce | 24 hours | order to delivery |
| Lead time, ambient categories | 72 hours | order to delivery |

## Cold chain

Chilled products (Dairy) must stay between **2°C and 6°C** from the supplier's dock to the store's chiller. Every chilled delivery must include a temperature log from a data logger. Stores must reject chilled deliveries without a log or with any reading above 8°C.

## Penalties

- For every full percentage point the monthly fill rate falls below 95%, the supplier pays a penalty of **1.5% of that month's invoice value**, capped at 10%.
- Quality failures that push a category's return rate above the 3% threshold in the Returns Policy are charged back to the supplier at cost, plus handling.

## Escalation

1. The buyer raises the issue with the supplier's account manager within 2 working days.
2. If it is not resolved within 10 working days, it goes to the Category Head and the supplier's commercial director.
3. Repeated failures in 3 consecutive months can lead to the supplier being put on probation or delisted.

## Scorecard

Each supplier gets a monthly scorecard covering fill rate, OTIF, quality returns and invoice accuracy. Scores below 80 out of 100 trigger a business review.
