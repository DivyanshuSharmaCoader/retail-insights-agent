# Kestrel Retail Q2 2026 Business Review (April - June)

*Kestrel Retail is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and exists to demonstrate retrieval.*

## Summary

Q2 2026 was the strongest quarter of the year so far. Net revenue grew across all four regions compared with Q1. The **South region grew fastest**, led by the Bengaluru hypermarket, helped by new residential catchments and the maturing Hyderabad express store.

## Category highlights

- **Beverages:** the **Summer Chill** campaign (15% off all beverages, 15 April - 31 May) was the standout campaign of the quarter. Combined with the normal summer peak, it comfortably beat the 20% uplift target set in the Promotions Playbook. Mango drinks, coconut water and mineral water sold best.
- **Snacks:** flat versus Q1. No promotion ran in Q2.
- **Fresh Produce:** steady, with a small seasonal lift in May and June.

## Issue: Dairy returns in May

In **May 2026 the Dairy return rate rose to above 6%**, more than double the 3% investigation threshold in the Returns Policy. Most returns were recorded as `expired` or `quality`.

The Quality Assurance investigation found that a **third-party cold-chain carrier serving Amrit Dairy** had a refrigeration fault on two of its trucks during the first heatwave of the year. Temperature logs showed readings above 8°C on several deliveries that stores should have rejected.

Actions taken:

1. Amrit Dairy submitted a corrective action plan and replaced the carrier from 1 June.
2. Penalties were applied under the Supplier SLA, and the quality chargeback was issued.
3. All stores repeated training on checking temperature logs at the receiving dock.
4. Wireless chiller sensors are being piloted in five stores.

Dairy return rates went back to normal (about 1%) in June.

## Priorities for Q3

- Launch Monsoon Home Saver (Household) and Fresh August (Fresh Produce).
- Bring Dairy Days forward to September to rebuild Dairy sales after the May issue.
- Complete the chiller sensor pilot and decide on a full rollout.
