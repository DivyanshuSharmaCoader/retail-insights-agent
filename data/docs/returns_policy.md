# Kestrel Retail Customer Returns Policy

*Kestrel Retail is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and exists to demonstrate retrieval.*

## Standard return window

Customers can return most products within **30 days** of purchase with the original receipt or the digital receipt in the Kestrel app. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Category-specific rules

- **Fresh Produce and Dairy:** returns are accepted within **48 hours** of purchase, and only for quality problems (spoiled, damaged or past its expiry date). Customers do not need to bring the product back for refunds under INR 200; a photo is enough.
- **Personal Care:** only unopened products in their original seal can be returned, for hygiene reasons. Opened products are accepted only if the product is faulty.
- **Household:** standard 30-day window. Hazardous products (for example toilet cleaner or mosquito coils) must be returned sealed.
- **Snacks and Beverages:** standard 30-day window for sealed products; damaged or expired items at any time with a receipt.

## Return reasons

Store staff record one reason for every return: `damaged`, `expired`, `quality`, `wrong_item` or `changed_mind`. These codes feed the returns dashboard and the supplier scorecard.

## Return-rate monitoring

The return rate is measured as **units returned divided by units sold, per category, per calendar month**.

- If any category's monthly return rate goes above **3%**, the Quality Assurance team must open an investigation within **5 working days**.
- The investigation checks supplier batches, cold-chain records (for chilled categories) and store handling.
- If the cause is a supplier, the supplier must submit a corrective action plan, and the issue is logged against them in the supplier scorecard (see the Supplier Service Level Agreement).
- Typical monthly return rates are about 1% for most categories and up to 2% for Fresh Produce.

## Fraud controls

More than 3 refunds without a receipt in 30 days for the same customer account triggers a manual review by the store manager.
