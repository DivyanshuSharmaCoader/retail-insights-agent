# Kestrel Retail Store Operations Handbook (extract)

*Kestrel Retail is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and exists to demonstrate retrieval.*

## Store formats

Kestrel runs three store formats:

- **Hypermarket:** more than 35,000 sq ft of selling area, full grocery and general merchandise range, open 9:00 to 23:00. Hypermarkets anchor each region.
- **Supermarket:** 12,000 to 25,000 sq ft, full grocery range with a limited general merchandise range, open 8:00 to 22:00.
- **Express:** under 8,000 sq ft, convenience range focused on top-up shopping, open 7:00 to 23:30.

The network covers four regions (North, South, East and West), each led by a Regional Operations Manager.

## Store KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Sales per sq ft (monthly) | set per format in the annual plan |
| Shrinkage (loss from damage, theft and expiry) | below **1.2%** of sales |
| On-shelf availability | at least **97%** of active SKUs |
| Checkout waiting time | under 4 minutes at peak |
| Customer satisfaction (NPS) | at least 45 |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** sales and availability review with the Regional Operations Manager.
- **Wednesday:** chiller and freezer temperature audit (Dairy and Fresh Produce).
- **Friday:** weekend readiness check: staffing, promotional displays and extra stock for high-demand lines. Weekends normally bring about 25% more sales than weekdays.

## Chilled goods handling

Store teams check chilled deliveries against the supplier temperature log before accepting them (see the Supplier SLA). Products from rejected deliveries must never be put on shelves. Chiller temperatures are recorded twice a day.

## New stores

A new store is treated as "non-comparable" for its first 12 months and is excluded from like-for-like growth figures.
